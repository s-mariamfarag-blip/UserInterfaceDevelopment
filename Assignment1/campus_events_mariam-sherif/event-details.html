Tech Innovation Fest 2026

Upcoming Technology

About the event

Tech Innovation Fest is a one-day showcase of student projects, short talks and hands-on workshops. Come to see what your classmates have built, meet industry guests and try new tools.

  • Date: 15 November 2026
  • Time: 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM
  • Venue: Main Auditorium, Block A
Register for this event

Agenda

  1. 10:00 AM - Welcome and opening talk
  2. 11:00 AM - Student project showcase
  3. 12:30 PM - Lunch break
  4. 1:30 PM - Web development workshop
  5. 3:00 PM - Industry panel and Q&A
  6. 3:45 PM - Awards and closing

Ticket and entry information

  • Entry is free for current students. Please bring your student ID.
  • Guest ticket: $5 at the door.
  • Seats are limited, so please register early.
  • The venue is wheelchair accessible.
